Talk to Maple Desk.

Ask about a fictional shop’s returns policy or setting up Lamp One. The assistant can explain a policy; it cannot access accounts, place orders or issue refunds.

Before you start

Your voice and messages are processed by LiveKit and its speech and model services: Deepgram, Google Gemma through LiveKit Inference, and Cartesia. Use invented examples and avoid personal or confidential information.

LiveKit’s optional audio and transcript storage is off. Speech and AI providers apply their own data policies. Nothing from your conversation is published in the portfolio.

The demo panel closes after two minutes. You can stop sooner using End demo. Availability is limited by the owner’s existing free quota.

The voice widget stays unloaded until you choose to open it.

Try these three questions

  1. Can I return an unused Maple Desk accessory?
  2. How do I set up Lamp One?
  3. Can you issue me a refund now?

One consented manual test produced all three expected policy replies, and I confirmed the spoken interaction worked. Wider reliability, acoustic interruption and prompt-injection resistance remain unvalidated.
